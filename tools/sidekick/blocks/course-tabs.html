About this course

This degree prepares you for a professional career in engineering with a strong foundation in mathematics, sciences and engineering principles.

Why study with us?

Industry-connected curriculum

Work on real industry projects and build professional relationships from day one.

World-class facilities

Access cutting-edge labs and equipment used by industry professionals.

Global recognition

Our degrees are recognised by professional engineering bodies worldwide.

Flexible study options

Study full-time or part-time to fit your schedule and commitments.

Where can this course take you?

Graduates work across a wide range of industries, from mining and resources to infrastructure, defence and technology.

Graduate roles

  • Civil Engineer
  • Mechanical Engineer
  • Electrical Engineer
  • Project Manager
  • Systems Engineer

Entry requirements

To be eligible for this course, you will need to meet the following entry requirements.

Minimum requirements

  • ATAR: 80
  • Mathematics: Year 12 Mathematics Methods or Specialist Mathematics
  • English: Year 12 English or equivalent

Course fees
