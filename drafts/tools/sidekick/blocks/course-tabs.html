About this Course

The Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) prepares graduates for a career in engineering through a rigorous combination of theory, design, and hands-on project work.

Students develop problem-solving skills and technical expertise demanded by Australian and global employers.

Why Study Engineering at Curtin?

Industry Connections

Access to 1,000+ employer partners and real-project internships from your first year.

World-Class Facilities

Work in state-of-the-art labs including our dedicated civil, structural and geotechnical testing spaces.

Global Recognition

Our programs are accredited by Engineers Australia and internationally recognised in over 20 countries.

Research-Informed Teaching

Learn from academics who are active researchers shaping the future of the profession.

Entry Requirements

To be eligible for this course, applicants must meet the following prerequisites and minimum score requirements.

Australian Applicants

  • ATAR: 80.00 (indicative)
  • Mathematics: WACE Mathematics Methods or Specialist Mathematics (or equivalent)
  • English: WACE English or Literature at a minimum mark of 50%

International Applicants

  • English proficiency: IELTS 6.5 overall (no band below 6.0)
  • Academic qualifications equivalent to Australian Year 12
