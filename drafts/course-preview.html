Major overview

Games are now one of the most influential forms of entertainment and interactive media worldwide. Used across a diverse range of industries, games combine art, design and technology to create immersive, captivating experiences.

In this major, you'll study game design and art in depth, learning how to create assets, design levels and bring game worlds to life.

Career outcomes

Graduates have the expertise for diverse, creative roles in platform development, coding puzzles, character animation, designing eSport athletes, composing gaming soundtracks or crafting storylines.

Careers

  • Game animation / cinematics artist
  • Game designer / environment artist
  • Game visual effects artist
  • 3D modeller / artist
  • 3D character designer
  • Level designer
